Grimhollow / v0.6 / Stage 5.5

Reference board
for human review.

45 numbered references, with 1K material maps and source licenses. Click any image for the unmodified downloaded file. These are studies of materials, light and silhouette; the game and art pipeline are unchanged.

Review the fit notes alongside each image. Several compliant equivalents cover part of a row’s qualities: a monochrome sewer supplies masonry and water geometry; museum objects supply shape; the burned-ground scan supplies scorch values. The original numeric targets have not been relaxed. Stage 5.6 has not begun.
