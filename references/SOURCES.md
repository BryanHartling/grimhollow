Retrieved 2026-09-08. All committed images are CC0 or public domain; 45 numbered references plus 10 companion files. No source pixels were edited, cropped, recolored or recompressed. Provider previews are identified by the exact download URL. Section B includes 1024×1024 diffuse, OpenGL normal, roughness and displacement maps for all three rows.

The board is a collection of qualities, not approved game art. Fit notes identify substitutions and missing visual qualities; measurable targets in `references.md` remain unchanged. An unlit albedo is not a wet shaded render, and photographic exposure is not a claim that a future game render passes the numeric targets. No rows are marked not found; all have a compliant source of the intended visual qualities.
